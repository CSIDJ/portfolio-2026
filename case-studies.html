Featured Projects

These use cases combine two goals: demonstrate a full project management chain (from ideation to validation) and express this approach for an AMOA / Digital Project Manager positioning.

Learn more

The setup was tested on simple projects (example: video) and complex ones (example: SaaS), with role/expertise logic assigned to preconfigured AI agents (OpenAI GPTs, Perplexity Projects, Gemini CLI, Claude, Roo Code, OpenRouter, Bolt.new), driven by continuously optimized custom instructions.

Only two use cases included human stakeholder requirements analysis: 🎬 Videos finder (V1) and 💳 POC Credit Manager (POC).

For all other use cases, collaboration was run as a single-sponsor setup with AI agents representing project roles (not operational human teams).
